WebRTC · peer-to-peer · open source

Chat and share files directly between browsers.

Your messages and files travel straight to the other browser. The server only introduces you, and never sees what you send.

  1. Join a room with any name.
  2. Open the invite link in another tab or on another device.
  3. Chat and send files. Try “Rebuild connection” mid-transfer: it resumes.
